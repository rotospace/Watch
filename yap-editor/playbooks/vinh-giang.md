# Playbook: engagement devices

Focus area: keeping attention across the middle of the video, where most drop-off
happens — the reasoning behind the "re-hook every 3–5s" rule in `SUMMARY.md`.

> Starter synthesis, not a transcript of any specific video. Replace with notes from
> whichever creator you actually study for this.

## Core idea
Attention decays continuously, not just at the start. A video needs small
re-engagement moments throughout — a pattern interrupt, a vocal shift, a callback —
not just one strong hook at 0:00.

## What to look for in a take
- Vocal variety: does pace/volume/pitch shift across the take, or is it one register
  the whole way through? Variety wins.
- Callbacks: does a later line reference the hook or an earlier beat? That's worth
  preserving even if it means keeping a slightly longer segment.
- Direct questions to camera ("you ever notice...") over flat statements.

## Editing implications
- Caption emphasis should land on the word carrying the point, not be applied
  uniformly — that's a caption-style rule, not just a transcription formatting one.
- If the middle third of a take is flat/same-energy for 8+ seconds with no device,
  prefer a different take's middle section if one exists, even if this take's hook or
  payoff is stronger — that's exactly the "best segment per beat" reconstruction.
