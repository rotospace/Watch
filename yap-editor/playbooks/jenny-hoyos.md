# Playbook: sub-60s story structure

Focus area: compressing a full story arc into well under a minute without it feeling
rushed — the reasoning behind the beat structure in `playbooks/SUMMARY.md`.

> Starter synthesis, not a transcript of any specific video. Replace with notes from
> whichever creator you actually study for this.

## Core idea
A sub-60s video still needs hook / stakes / escalation / payoff / CTA — it just gets
1–2 seconds per beat instead of 10. The arc doesn't get cut, the fat around it does.

## Beat budget (default, tune per video)
- Hook: 0:00–0:03
- Stakes: 0:03–0:10
- Build: the middle, one idea, no detours
- Payoff: final beat, never trimmed
- CTA: last 2–3 seconds, one instruction

## Editing implications
- If a take spends more than ~10s establishing context before the first real claim,
  that's a candidate for a hard cut, not a slow trim.
- The payoff should land with silence or a clean caption right after it — don't stack
  the CTA directly onto the punchline with no breathing room.
- Total runtime is a symptom, not a target: enforce the beat budget, let the runtime
  fall out of that.
